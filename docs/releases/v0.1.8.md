# PGRazor v0.1.8

## New features

- **Automation > Send & Open Gifts:** start one batch to send gifts to eligible friends, then open available gifts. Progress reflects confirmed responses; Stop cancels unsent work. The full-bag option attempts opening using the game's reward preference. Eligibility, inventory and daily limits still apply.
- **Location & overlays > Customize Quick Menu:** choose which entries appear without enabling or disabling their features. Choices are saved per account and included in backups. Settings always remains accessible.
- **Gyms, Raids & Dynamax:** separate Gyms, Raids and Dynamax groups. Gym Slotter & Berry Feeder appears first inside Gyms, and existing battle choices are preserved.

## Fixes and improvements

- Restore automatic opening and sending from friend screens.
- Preserve profile scroll position during VGP+ catches, transfers and player-stat updates.
- Reduce VGP+ idle scene scans from up to 10 per second to 1 per second while retaining responsive pending catches and cancellation.
- Make buddy care work behind menus with less overhead: idle checks run once per second instead of every 100 ms, with one scene lookup per evaluation and berry-selection service lookups reduced from up to five to one.
- Recover map readiness after a temporary unusable camera heading.
- Update the website's feature guides and settings paths.

## Verification and limitations

- Android gift-batch testing confirmed nine gifts sent, eight opened, and Stop behavior. Full-bag reward conversion has not been verified live.
- Buddy feeding, playing, snapshot and gift actions behind menus were confirmed by the user.
- Quick Menu visibility, persistence after restart and unchanged feature settings were checked on Android.
- Intermittent Android Unity/JNI startup crashes remain unresolved; this release does not claim a startup-crash fix.
- The live Daily Incense Galarian-bird encounter handoff remains unverified. Catch manually; no automatic throw or Master Ball path is provided.
- The reduced VGP+ scan rate is a performance improvement, not a verified fix for reports of disappearing spawns.

## Installation

Install **PGRazor.apk** over your existing installation to preserve settings. One APK supports ARM64 Android phones and MuMu. **SHA256SUMS.txt** provides the checksum, and **build-info.json** identifies the source build.
