# PGRazor v0.2.0

## New features

- **Research:** Auto-claims completed field research and auto-deletes the field research tasks you pick from a list grouped into Item, Encounter and Event. It can also catch waiting field research encounters without the encounter screen, using your VGP+ ball and throw settings and your transfer rules. Open it from the quick menu.
- **Storage list IVs:** Shows each Pokémon's IVs under its CP in the Pokémon storage list, as attack/defense/HP, a percentage, or both, colored like the encounter IV overlay. All IV display settings are now together under Settings > Location & overlays > IV Display.
- **Hide overlay:** A quick menu entry that makes the game look stock after a confirmation, hiding every PGRazor button, status bar and message plus the in-game IV text. Tap the screen with three fingers at once to show them again.
- **GPX route start:** Starting a GPX route in Autowalk asks where to begin: from the closest point on the route, from the beginning, or by teleporting to the beginning.
- **Copy coordinates:** The World map has Copy my location and Copy pin location buttons that center the map on that spot and copy its coordinates. The Teleport dialog shows your current coordinates with Copy and Favorite buttons.

## Improvements & fixes

- The encounter IV overlay now also shows on field research reward encounters.
- Always Hit now works when catching after a Max Battle.
- Wild catches now get the AR+ catch bonus.
- Menu options and buttons now look like buttons.
