# PGRazor v0.3.5.2 (PGRazor_Pokematrix fork)

Built on PGRazor v0.3.5 with all v0.3.5.1 fork changes, plus:

## New features

- **TM shortcuts on the Pokémon screen.** A Fast TM / Charged TM icon sits before each move's power number on both move tabs. Tapping it starts the game's own TM flow, so the game's confirmation still appears. With no TM of that kind the icon is grey and a tap says so. Toggle: Settings › IV display › "TM shortcuts on the Pokémon screen".
- **VGP+ tag rules.** "Catch only" and "Candy species" can use your Pokémon storage tags (Browse by category › "Pokémon storage tags"). A tag covers the whole candy family (a tagged Blastoise covers Squirtle; regional forms share candy) and follows your tags as you change them in the game.
  - "Candy species berry" (Pinap by default) for candy species.
  - "Rare Pokémon: Ultra Ball + rare berry" replaces "Shiny or IV ≥90%". Choose what counts as rare (Legendary/Mythical/Ultra Beast, shiny, shundo, 100%, nundo, IV threshold, event costume, special background, an ultra-rare species list) and the berry (Silver Pinap, Golden Razz…).
  - "Also catch rare Pokémon outside Catch only".
  - Overlay button (Pinap / silver Pinap) to switch the tag rules on and off.
- **Export Pokémon for CalcyIV** (experimental). Settings › Tools writes your whole storage as a CSV for CalcyIV's "Import History from CSV". CalcyIV skips Pokémon it already has. Regional and special forms may be rejected.

## Fixes

- VGP+ never saw a wild Pokémon's IVs (the map's copy has none), so "Shiny or IV ≥90%" could not match on IV. It now reads the encounter response, as Shundo Sniper does.
- The IV line on the Pokémon screen was drawn behind the 3D model when the screen was opened from the Pokédex.
- The IV line kept the old level after a power-up.
- VGP stuck on "Waiting for game readiness" with location spoofing off: the real location is now also taken from the location updates the game receives (not verified on the affected devices).
- After changing VGP+ tag rules, Pokémon skipped under the old rules were ignored for an hour; they are now considered again at once.
