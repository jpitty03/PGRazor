# PGRazor v0.2.3

## New features

- **Battery usage:** Lower the game's resolution, cap it at 20 FPS, or turn off effects such as shadows and fog. Find it under Settings > General Settings.
- **Throw variety:** Each throw gets a random rating from the ones you pick: Excellent & Great; Excellent, Great & Nice; or All, which includes a plain throw. Available under Throws and in VGP+. Off by default.
- **VGP+ catch-only list:** Choose species, and VGP+ catches only those and leaves everything else on the map.
- **Shundo Sniper black screen:** Tap Dark on its card to black out the game while it runs. Tap the screen to bring the game back.

## Improvements & fixes

- Smart healing: new Items choice. Always heal fully uses whatever potions and revives it takes.
- Shundo Sniper: starts sooner, moves on faster when its target doesn't appear, and shows missed checks on its card.
- Transfer on catch: new "Keep 0% IV (0/0/0)" option.
- Gym Slotter and Berry Feeder: placing a defender now comes before feeding.
- World map tiles load faster.
- More cutscene skips: level-up screen, medal pop-ups and the weekly Adventure Sync summary.
