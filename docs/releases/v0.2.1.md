# PGRazor v0.2.1

## New features

- **Catch chance and auto ball:** Manual encounters show each ball's chance to catch, using the server's catch rate with your berry, throw and curveball. Auto ball can pick the cheapest Poké, Great or Ultra Ball that reaches your target chance; set both under Settings > Throws.
- **VGP+ auto ball:** VGP+ can pick the cheapest ball that reaches your target chance for each throw. Your shiny, IV and CP/level Ultra Ball rules still apply, and it never uses a Master Ball.
- **VGP incense catches:** Virtual GO Plus and VGP+ now also catch Pokémon attracted by your incense.
- **PvP keep:** Auto-transfer can keep catches with good PvP IVs, with a separate rank for Great League (default top 50), Ultra League (default top 10) and Master League (off by default). Only species in PvPoke's top 150 for the league count. Set it under Transfer on catch.
- **PvP IV display:** A new PvP IV format adds a row with each league's crest and rank to the encounter, Pokémon stats screen and storage list.
- **IV display:** Encounter and stats-screen IVs now show on a light panel, placed just above the name on the stats screen. Each screen has one button that cycles through the formats under Settings > Location & overlays > IV Display.
- **PoGoCoordinates feed:** Feeds now lets you choose LivePokeMap or PoGoCoordinates. PoGoCoordinates searches Pokémon, raids, quests, weather, Rocket and power spots, with Discord sign-in for more results per search.
- **LivePokeMap PvP search:** The new PvP only filter lists just Pokémon within your max rank in the leagues you tick, such as Great League top 50. Feed rows show any top-100 league rank.

## Improvements & fixes

- The quick menu and draggable overlays stay inside the screen, clear of the notch and navigation bar.
- LivePokeMap's My Shinies tab is removed, since LivePokeMap no longer offers it.
