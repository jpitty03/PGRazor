# PGRazor v0.2.4

## New features

- **Smart battling:** Picks counters and builds your team for gym, raid, and Team GO Rocket grunt battles. It also swaps to a suitable counter during gym and grunt battles; off by default.
- **Evolve before transferring:** Evolve new catches once or through all available stages before transferring. Apply your current transfer rules or choose a separate species list; existing keep protections still apply.
- **Gym battles:** The new Battle until defeated toggle repeats full rounds from the gym screen. Smart healing can heal your team between rounds; off by default.
- **Local time:** An optional draggable clock in General Settings shows the time at your spoofed location.

## Improvements & fixes

- Updated for Pokémon GO 0.435.2.
- Max Battles automatically move your Pokémon toward spawned Max energy orbs.
- Improved Smart battling team selection and healing-threshold handling.
- Improved raid cutscene progression, level-up readiness, and gym badge popup dismissal.
- Adventure Sync prompts are dismissed while location spoofing is enabled.
- Added post-hatch Eggs-page handling for Auto incubate while preserving manual access.
- The In-Game Routes quick-menu icon now matches the Routes overlay and the menu's icon color.
- Various UI improvements and minor fixes.
