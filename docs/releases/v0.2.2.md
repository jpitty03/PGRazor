# PGRazor v0.2.2

## New features

- **Shundo Sniper (experimental):** Teleports to 100% IV Pokémon from the PGRazor 100% IV feed (no sign-in needed, you're welcome you greedy bastards), LivePokeMap and PoGoCoordinates, checks each one in the background for shiny and catches only shinies. A shiny found during a cooldown is held until the cooldown ends, then caught. Open it from Automation. Its constant teleporting raises the risk of an account warning or ban, so use an alt account.
- **Skip cutscenes:** Skips evolution, egg hatch and other Pokémon cutscenes, Team GO Rocket and Giovanni dialogue, battle and raid result screens, and the power-up animation. Off by default; turn it on under Settings > General Settings.
- **Smart healing:** Heals damaged Pokémon after battles, periodically, or both, choosing the mix of potions (and optionally revives) that wastes the least. Choose the HP threshold under Settings > Team Rocket, Gyms, Raids & Dynamax.

## Improvements & fixes

- Team GO Rocket battles: while any Rocket automation option is on, the next Pokémon is picked automatically when one faints.
- World map: PokéStops invaded by Team GO Rocket show darkened with a red R.
- Skip cutscenes also closes the "You're going too fast" warning.
- Teleports and walking no longer stop moving you on the map after the game switches between its location services.
- Research: a task whose deletion fails is tried again instead of staying until the game restarts.
- Location & overlays is renamed General Settings, and Team Rocket settings now sit under Team Rocket, Gyms, Raids & Dynamax.
