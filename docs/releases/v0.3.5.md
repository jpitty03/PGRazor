# PGRazor v0.3.5

## New features

- **Exchange gifts with Mateo:** on a Route, PGRazor taps Mateo once you are in range and completes his gift exchange for you. Pokémon trades are left to you. Turn it on under Go Plus, Stops & Items → Routes.
- **Settings search:** a search field at the top of Settings finds any setting by name, description or common keyword, then opens its page and scrolls to it.
- **Redesigned interface:** settings, the quick menu, overlays, dialogs and the map share one calmer look, and the settings home is grouped into Play, Automation and App.

## Improvements & fixes

- Auto-Raid uses a newly awarded free raid pass instead of stopping when the previous one is spent.
- Routes you walk yourself no longer pause with "your location could not be established".
- The joystick no longer snaps back to the middle while you hold it.
- Map catches now follow your auto-transfer rules.
- Settings stay a comfortable width on tablets, and status bar icons follow the light or dark theme.
